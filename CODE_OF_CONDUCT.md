# Code of Conduct

## My commitment

I want LibreLeaf to be a welcoming, practical project where people can contribute regardless of experience, identity, background, or access needs. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help create an environment free from harassment and intimidation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions constructively.
- Respect privacy, attribution, accessibility, and lawful access.
- Accept moderation decisions and step back when asked.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualised attention
- Insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Encouraging infringement, evasion, deceptive downloads, or unsafe behaviour through this project
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing LibreLeaf.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to **max@mybrim.io**. Please include links, dates, and enough context to understand what happened. Do not put sensitive personal information into a public issue.

I will review reports as promptly and impartially as I can, protect the privacy of those involved where possible, and take proportionate action. Actions may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Deliberately false or abusive reports may themselves be treated as a breach of this code.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), adapted into shorter project-specific language.
